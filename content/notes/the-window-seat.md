Second note in the stream, so the spread mirrors: these words take
the left side and the picture takes the right.

![A tall view from the window](/static/art/the-window-seat/1.jpg)

The alternation is the whole interface. No cards, no thumbnails, no
"read more" — the rhythm of left-right-left tells you where one note
ends and the next begins, the way facing pages do in a book.

A portrait image works the same as a landscape one: it fills its
whole side, cropped to fit, never letterboxed. Keep scrolling for the
third note, which swaps back.
