This is the newest note, so it opens the stream. Its image holds the
left side while these paragraphs scroll on the right.

![Hills under a winter sky](/static/art/late-january/1.jpg)

A note is a small thing — an observation, a link, a sentence that
wouldn't leave. In the stream you read them one after another, the
way you'd flip through a notebook, instead of choosing from a list.

Each note still has its own page and its own place in the RSS feed;
the stream is just how the section reads. Scroll on — the next note
swaps sides.
